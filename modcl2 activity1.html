ABOUT SUN

Little bit about me.

Size and Structure: The Sun is about 100 times wider than Earth and contains 99.86% of the total mass of the entire solar system. Distance and Light: It is about 93 miles (150 million kilometers) from Earth; sunlight takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach us. Gravity: The Sun’s immense gravity holds the solar system together, keeping planets, asteroids, and comets in orbit. Rotation: The Sun rotates faster at its equator (about 25 Earth days) than at its poles (about 34-36 Earth days). Energy Source: Through nuclear fusion, the Sun converts hydrogen into helium, releasing vast amounts of energy. Active Star: The Sun has a magnetic field that creates sunspots, flares, and coronal mass ejections.
